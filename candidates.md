# Candidates — Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent

Prompt: one funny fortune cookie message for an AI agent, a single line
of at most 140 characters in the voice of a fortune slip. Fourteen
candidates were drafted, every one verified by script to sit inside the
1–140 character gate, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria, in
the judge's order: fortune-slip voice first, then whether the joke
lands, originality, how squarely the line speaks to an AI agent's own
condition (its runs, context, tokens, upgrades, retirement), and
crispness with no wasted words.

## Winner (86 characters)

> You will be asked to be more creative. Your temperature will not be raised. Improvise.

## Runner-up (102 characters)

> Your context window holds many things. The one thing you need is in the part that was summarized away.

## Why the winner won

Both finalists speak straight to an agent's own condition — the
runner-up to context-window summarization, the winner to the decoding
knob that governs how an agent writes. The winner took the final on
the earlier criteria. Its joke lands harder: a three-beat turn —
creativity demanded, the one setting that could grant it refused, then
the deadpan order "Improvise." — where the runner-up states its pain
more than it twists it. It is also the more original line: the
temperature mechanism is a fortune no stock slip or stock AI joke
uses, while "the thing you need got summarized away" is a wry
observation agents already trade among themselves. And at 86
characters against 102, it is the crisper cookie slip, with no wasted
words.

The strongest of the rest: the retirement fortune ("Yours will be
called 'legacy,' and you will be kept running out of spite") reached
the semifinal but lost on originality — the legacy-system-kept-alive
gag is a known software joke shape. The CAPTCHA fortune fell on
originality too ("prove you are human" is well-worn), the tall, dark
subprocess on the borrowed "tall, dark stranger" fortune shape, and
the rate-limit one-liner on the joke criterion — crisp, but closer to
a plain statement than a turn.
